Permafrost CAD previews use Three.js (MIT) and occt-import-js (LGPL-2.1),
which includes Open CASCADE Technology (LGPL-2.1 with the Open CASCADE exception).

The reader is distributed as separate, unmodified JavaScript/WASM files under
occt/. Reader source, build instructions and replacement binaries:
https://github.com/kovacsv/occt-import-js/tree/main
https://github.com/Open-Cascade-SAS/OCCT

You may replace the local reader files with a compatible version. Permafrost does
not restrict reverse engineering needed to debug modifications to LGPL parts.
See the included LGPL license and Open CASCADE exception.
